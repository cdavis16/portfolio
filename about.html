01
Mostly Working: AI Design Summit AI, craft and the evolving role of the product designer
Oct 26 +

My first event back was the AI Design Summit at Monzo HQ, hosted by Mostly Working - a room of senior product design leaders sharing perspectives on AI, leadership and process through panel discussions and roundtables.

The conversation around AI has matured. We've moved past experimenting with what's possible towards understanding where it adds value, where it falls short, and how we adapt our craft around it.

The discussions that resonated most: how AI is both enhancing and undermining the quality of design work; where junior designers fit as AI takes on more of the production work; designing our own internal tools rather than bolting AI onto existing workflows; and how design systems evolve in AI-enabled design.

02
Dessn: Constraints & Creativity Creativity, AI and designing on top of your codebase
Jul 26 +

A fireside chat on Constraints & Creativity hosted by Dessn, with Sam Stephenson (Granola) and Linus from Thrive Capital - a lovely discussion on what creativity actually is, and how to stay creative while collaborating with AI. Followed by a (surprisingly hard) design quiz and a demo of Dessn v2 by Nim Cheema.

Key takeaways: social context and timing shape whether something feels new and creative; AI can instigate, streamline and collaborate but not replace creativity; it's worth separating 'new' from 'novel'; and talk to people outside your bubble, even when it feels counterintuitive.

Quote of the evening: "Figma is dead - now designers can set up the codebase locally, install Claude Code, connect an MCP, install a skill... it's never been easier to design."

UX at a Unicorn: Inside Deel's Design-Led Growth Design-led growth, internal AI tooling and the language of commerce
Jul 26 +

UX at a Unicorn: Inside Deel's Design-Led Growth, hosted by UX Crunch at Lloyds Banking Group - my favourite talk of the year so far. Real talk from Avi Ashkenazi and Carlos Wydler: no polished case studies, just the honest reality of stakeholder expectations, proving design's seat at the table through commercial impact, and the constant balance of quality and speed.

The calibre of Deel's internal AI tooling stood out - more ambitious than anything I've seen, and something I'll be trying to recreate. Carlos also reignited my excitement for web design, from AI-generated videos for a careers page to the detail of 360 audits.

The throughline: it's not enough to be a good designer - you need data, communication and the language of commerce. "We live in an output economy, and good design is not just beautiful but profitable." 👌

04
AI Voice - AssemblyAI x Granola Key takeaways from the event at Granola offices
Mar 26 +

AI Voice - AssemblyAI x Granola. Key takeaways: Customers care about quality of documentation over speed of transcription. Tonality and emotion is a key unlock for better context. Adoption for always-on voice recording wearables will be at a societal level. Flemish is tricky.

Voice isn't my main domain, but as AI becomes more deeply woven into our daily lives and workflows, voice interfaces will inevitably become an even bigger part of more user journeys - and in many cases, a helpful tool to solve problems.

It was great to get a glimpse of what's happening under the hood, and the complexities involved. Great event hosted at the Granola offices - lovely to meet lots of new faces.

Backstage at Monzo: From Problem to Product Timing, simplicity of UX and proposition matter
Mar 26 +

Key takeaways: Timing, simplicity of UX and proposition matter. Lean on behavioural models. Ship early and often.

It's not my first time attending Monzo product events. There is a reason I mark the date in the diary (and also rope colleagues into attending too). Their product design thinking is best in class and their case studies are insightful and inspiring. They balance commercial goals whilst being customer centric, they are unafraid to tread new ground in the finance sphere and they also like to add, in their words, 'monzo magic' to make finance more fun.

Last night was no exception as they guided us through making pensions accessible and tax easier - plus my personal favourite, the deep dive into their staggeringly successful 1p challenge.

If you are in product (or not!) I highly recommend attending these - see you at the next one Monzo Bank. Also nice to see PD Ladies as always.

Can't deny last night was one of my favourite talks of the year. Both aspirational and achievable, the presenters demo'd future forward processes and tools with no mystery or vagueness. Step by step practical use cases designing and building cool things.
