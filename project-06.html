Overview

An app I've designed and built, currently in Beta (link available on request). Spurr solves a familiar problem for runners: need mid-race motivation, only to miss your friends at the sideline, or to have family who couldn't make it there at all. Supporters record voice messages through a unique share link generated for each race, and those voicenotes play back at distance milestones the runner chooses during their race.

Platform

Mobile app

Tools

Claude Code · Midjourney · Supabase

Status

Beta

01

Create a race

Onboarding

The onboarding flow walks a runner through setting up their race, naming the run, choosing a distance, and picking race day, then creating an account to generate their unique share link.

02

Invite

Share

Once a race is set up, the runner shares their unique link. They can share a variety of ways, and include their own unique message alongside.

03

Web

Share link

The web experience friends and family land on when they open the link is a simple flow to record and send a voice message for the runner, no app required.

04

Set up

Assign Spurrs to Milestones

The runner assigns each recorded Spurr to a distance milestone via a simple drag and drop, similar to the edit playlist pattern on Spotify. The user chooses exactly where along the route each voice message will play during their race.

05

The run

Race day

On race day, the race is unlocked and the runner starts their race, just like they would a Strava run. Each Spurr plays automatically at its chosen milestone, with a countdown display so the user knows exactly when their next Spurr will be.
